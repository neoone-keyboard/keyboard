Type your language the way you say it.

An iPhone keyboard for English and for languages you type the way they sound: write nanni and get നന്ദി, dhanyavaad and get धन्यवाद.

What it does

  • Suggestions, autocorrect and next-word prediction in English and in each language you add.
  • Phonetic typing: type in Latin letters and pick the native-script word from the bar.
  • Native layouts for each language, swipe typing, and emoji.
  • Up to four languages at once, with a language key to switch between them.

Private by design

Everything runs on your iPhone. The keyboard needs no Full Access, has no network access, and keeps the words it learns on the phone. Read the privacy policy.

Languages

English is built in. Malayalam, Hindi and Assamese are free downloads from inside the app, and each is a few tens of megabytes. More languages can be added as packs.
