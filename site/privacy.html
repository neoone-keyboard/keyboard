Privacy

NeoOne Keyboard collects no data. Nothing you type leaves your iPhone.

The keyboard

  • The keyboard works offline. It has no network access and does not ask for Full Access (“Allow Full Access” stays off).
  • Suggestions, autocorrect, phonetic typing, swipe and emoji all run on the phone.
  • The words it learns from your typing are stored on your iPhone, in the keyboard’s own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The app and the network

The NeoOne Keyboard app uses the network for one thing: downloading the language packs you pick. When you open its language screens it fetches the list of packs from this site (https://neoone.jishnu.me/packs/index.json), and when you tap Get or Update it downloads that pack from this site too. These requests carry no information about you or what you type. As with any web request, the servers see your IP address; this site and the downloads are hosted by Cloudflare, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

You can also add a pack from the Files app, without any network access.

Crash reports

The app saves Apple’s performance and crash reports (MetricKit) on the phone and does not upload them. If you chose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share crash reports with us under Apple’s terms; they do not contain what you type.

This website

This site sets no cookies and loads no scripts, fonts or trackers.
